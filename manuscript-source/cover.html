Budget-split deep ensembles outperform snapshot ensembles and weight averaging for calibrated uncertainty
Under a fixed training budget of 60 SGD epochs on Fashion-MNIST, splitting the budget across three independent 20-epoch restarts gave the lowest test NLL (0.302 vs 0.527 for a single long-trained network; Δ = −0.225, 95% CI [−0.236, −0.213], Holm-adjusted P = 0.0098, n = 10 seeds), the best calibration, and the strongest OOD detection. Weight averaging of the same checkpoints improved in-distribution metrics but showed no evidence of improving OOD detection (Δ AUROC = −0.003, P = 0.64).
